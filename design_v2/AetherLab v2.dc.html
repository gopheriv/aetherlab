Damped harmonic oscillator COMPLETE
∑
Governing equation
$$m\ddot{x} + c\dot{x} + kx = 0$$
1
Assume a trial solution $x(t)=e^{\lambda t}$, giving the characteristic equation $m\lambda^2 + c\lambda + k = 0$.
2
Solve for the roots: $\lambda = \dfrac{-c \pm \sqrt{c^2 - 4mk}}{2m}$. The discriminant sets the regime.
3
Define natural frequency and damping ratio; for $\zeta<1$ the motion is underdamped with decaying oscillation $x(t)=A e^{-\zeta\omega_0 t}\cos(\omega_d t+\varphi)$.
Key results
$\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$
$\zeta=\dfrac{c}{2\sqrt{mk}}$
$\omega_d=\omega_0\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}$
